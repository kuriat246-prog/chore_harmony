💡 Quick Guide: How to get your Google App Password (Takes 1 min)

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/security.
  2. Make sure 2-Step Verification is turned ON.
  3. Type "App passwords" in the top search bar and click it.
  4. Enter any name (e.g. ChoreHarmony) and click Create.
  5. Copy the 16-letter code generated by Google and paste it above into Google App Password!
